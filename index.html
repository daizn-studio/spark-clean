FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What's included in an end of lease clean?
All rooms cleaned and surfaces wiped, floors vacuumed and mopped, kitchen cleaned inside and out including cupboards and appliance exteriors, bathrooms scrubbed including tiles, shower, bath and toilet, plus commonly-missed detail like skirting boards, light switches and window sills. A standard oven clean (inside, racks, stovetop and rangehood) is included. Carpet steam cleaning and heavy-duty oven cleaning for baked-on grease are extras you select when quoting.
Do you guarantee I'll get my bond back?
We guarantee rectification, not the bond itself — an important distinction. If your agent flags something inside the scope you booked, we return and fix it free within 72 hours. We can't guarantee the bond outcome, because that also depends on property damage, wear and tear, and other matters unrelated to cleaning.
How is this different from other Melbourne cleaners?
Most quote by phone or a callback form. You get an itemised price here in about 60 seconds, including GST, and can book and pay immediately. The trade-off is honest: our estimate is subject to on-site verification, so if the property differs materially from what you entered, the price can be adjusted — with the reason stated.
When should I book?
Ideally about a week before your lease ends, once you've moved most belongings out. Cleaners work far more effectively in an empty property, and it leaves room for touch-ups before the final inspection.
What won't you take on?
Heavy mould, biological waste, deep odour or smoke damage, heavy wall markings needing repainting-level work, and chemical or deep pet-urine carpet treatment. These need specialised remediation equipment we don't deploy, so we refer you to a restoration partner rather than take a job we can't do properly.
Do you service my suburb?
Greater Melbourne only for now. Sydney, Brisbane, Perth and Adelaide are planned. Enter your suburb when quoting and we'll route the job to a vetted local cleaner.
How do I check a booking I've already made?
Use "Track my booking" on the booking page and enter the phone number you booked with. You'll see your status and confirmed time once management has verified it.
