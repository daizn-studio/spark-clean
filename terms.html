3. The Bond-Back Rectification Clause (residential end-of-lease cleans)

For End of Lease / Vacate cleans, the following applies:

  • Payment to the subcontractor is contingent on the property's bond being cleared by the managing real estate agent or landlord, where the clean is the basis for that clearance.
  • If the agent identifies an item genuinely within the booked scope that was missed, the subcontractor will return to rectify it at no additional cost to you, provided the request is raised within a reasonable timeframe (recommended: within 72 hours of the clean).
  • This clause covers items within the original booked scope only — it does not extend to the exclusions listed in clause 4, nor to matters unrelated to cleaning (e.g. property damage, maintenance issues, disputes over the bond amount itself).
  • Rectification is not owed if the shortfall was caused by conditions not disclosed at booking (e.g. undisclosed heavy soiling covered by clause 4) or by activity in the property after the clean was completed.

4. What's excluded from every standard quote

Standard quotes — residential and commercial — do not include:

  • Heavy wall markings: scuffs, ink, crayon, or paint damage requiring repainting-level work.
  • Chemical treatment or deep pet-urine carpet stains beyond standard steam cleaning.
  • Deep biological or atmospheric odour removal, including mould remediation and smoke damage mitigation.

If any of the above are identified — either during quoting (the "heavy-duty" screening question) or discovered on-site — we will not proceed with a standard clean for that item and may refer you to a specialised restoration partner instead.
